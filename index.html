Breezy - an Arduino Weather Station Project

This project aims to gather wind information from an Arduino-based weather station.

Overview

Having accurate information about local weather conditions is important for keeping people safe. Inspired by airport Automated Weather Observing Systems (AWOS) and the National Weather Service’s SKYWARN Storm Spotter Program, our project aims to build a weather station that measures and reports conditions to support informed decisions in aviation and severe weather preparedness.

Conditions — Last 60 Minutes

Each box shows the average over the last 60 minutes of readings, except Peak Gust, which shows the highest gust in the last 60 minutes.

How wind is measured (same method as airport ASOS stations): every 5 seconds the station turns the anemometer pulses into one 5-second average wind speed. Wind speed is the 2-minute average of the most recent 24 five-second averages. A gust is a single 5-second average; each 20-second upload reports the highest one in those 20 seconds, and the Peak Gust box shows the highest over the last hour.

Wind speed is being prioritized for validation and error analysis. Temperature, humidity and pressure readings have not been validated yet.
