Wind is the 2-minute average and gust is the highest 5-second average (ASOS method). In hourly view, wind, temperature, humidity, pressure and dew point are averaged over each hour and gust is the highest in that hour. Gaps in the lines are times the station was not uploading. Drag across a plot to zoom, double-click to zoom back out, and use the buttons above the plots to jump to the last day, 3 days or week.
